# ELECTRONIC ASSET 2026
**Date:** 22nd October 2026  
**Organized By:** Department of Electronics and Telecommunication (Tele-Era Department Student Forum, IETE Student Forum, and IIC CELL)  
**Objective:** To provide a competitive platform for students to showcase their technical prowess, innovative thinking, and presentation skills.

---

## 1. Project Competition
* **Eligibility:** VII Semester Students Only
* **Team Size:** Maximum 4 members per team
* **Approved Domains:** DSD, WSN, ES, DSP, MWR
* **Format:** Single-Round Exhibition & Live Evaluation

### Event Flow (Single-Round Execution)
* **09:00 AM - 10:00 AM (Reporting & Setup):** Teams arrive at the designated venue, verify their registration with the event coordinators, and set up their hardware/software models on their assigned workstations.
* **10:00 AM - 01:00 PM (Live Exhibition & Panel Evaluation):** The judging panel visits each workstation consecutively. Teams are allotted a strict 10-minute evaluation window, divided into a 7-minute technical demonstration and a 3-minute Q&A session.
* **01:00 PM - 02:00 PM (Score Compilation):** Judges finalize the scoring matrix. Teams dismantle their setups and clear their workstations.
* **02:00 PM (Result Declaration):** The top-scoring teams are announced based on the judges' cumulative evaluations.

### Rules & Regulations
1. **Setup & Hardware Requirements:** Teams must bring all necessary hardware components, laptops, and extension cords required for their project. The department will only provide a standard desk and a single electrical power socket. Projects must be fully functional.
2. **Domain Strictness:** The core technology of the project must strictly fall under one of the officially specified domains. Projects outside these domains will not be evaluated.
3. **Presentation & Conduct:** Teams must display a printed A3 poster or a technical synopsis chart at their desk. Every registered team member must be present. Formal attire or college uniform is strictly mandatory.
4. **Evaluation Matrix (100 Points Total):**
   * Innovation & Originality (25 points)
   * Technical Complexity & Execution (30 points)
   * Practical Applicability (20 points)
   * Presentation & Viva (25 points)
5. **Disqualification:** Submitting a purchased, pre-built commercial project, presenting a copied open-source project without significant modifications, or reusing a previous year's project will result in immediate disqualification.

## 2. PPT Presentation Competition
* **Eligibility:** VII Semester Students Only
* **Team Size:** Maximum 4 members per team
* **Venue:** Meeting Room
* **Format:** Single-Round Live Presentation

### Event Flow (Single-Round Execution)
* **11:00 AM (Reporting & File Transfer):** All participating teams must report strictly at 11:00 AM outside the Meeting Room. Teams will submit their pre-made presentation files (via pen drive) to the event coordinators.
* **11:30 AM Onwards (Live Presentations):** Teams will be called in sequentially to deliver their presentations before the judging panel.
* **Post-Presentation:** Teams must leave the immediate vicinity after their presentation to allow the next team to present seamlessly.

### Rules & Regulations
1. **Presentation Requirements:** Presentations must be completely pre-made prior to the event. The content presented must be highly original. Plagiarism will result in immediate disqualification.
2. **Discipline & Decorum:** Strict silence must be maintained outside the Meeting Room. Participants are strictly prohibited from gathering, loitering, or creating noise in the corridors.
3. **Presentation Timing:** Teams will be given a maximum of 8 minutes to present their slides, followed by a 2-minute Q&A session.
4. **Evaluation Matrix (20 Marks Total):** 4 criteria, each carrying a maximum of 5 marks:
   * Originality & Research (5 marks)
   * Slide Design & Visuals (5 marks)
   * Communication & Delivery (5 marks)
   * Q&A Defense (5 marks)

## 3. Bread Board Competition
* **Eligibility:** V Semester Students Only
* **Team Size:** Maximum 2 members per team
* **Venue:** EW's Lab
* **Format:** Two-Round Elimination

### Event Flow
* **Round 1 (Qualifying Round - Circuit on Paper):** Each team will be provided with a specific technical problem statement to solve by designing the correct circuit diagram on paper (Time limit: 15 to 30 minutes). Every team that successfully draws the functionally correct circuit will qualify for Round 2.
* **Round 2 (Practical Implementation):** Qualified teams will be given a completely new problem statement to design and physically construct a working circuit on a breadboard.

### Rules & Regulations
1. **Strict Anti-Cheating Policy:** Cheating in any form (mobile phones, unauthorized reference materials, communicating with other teams) is strictly prohibited. Caught teams face immediate disqualification.
2. **Component & Workspace Rules:** Teams must carefully handle all lab equipment and return all components properly after evaluation.
3. **Round 2 Evaluation Criteria:** Winners are decided based on successful execution of the required output. In case of a tie, neatness of wiring, optimization of components, and time taken will be considered.

## 4. Ideathon
* **Eligibility:** All Semesters
* **Team Size:** Maximum 4 members per team
* **Venue:** Aeronautical Seminar Hall (Room 303)
* **Format:** On-the-spot Problem Solving, Mentorship Filtering, and Final Presentation

### Event Flow
* **10:00 AM (Reporting):** Teams report to the venue for attendance and seating.
* **10:30 AM (Problem Statement Reveal):** The official problem statements/themes are revealed.
* **10:30 AM - 01:00 PM (Brainstorming & Solution Design):** Teams work collaboratively to develop an innovative solution or business model.
* **01:00 PM Onwards (Mentor Round):** A mentor or judge visits each team. Teams pitch their raw concept. Mentors shortlist the most promising teams.
* **Final Presentation:** Shortlisted teams proceed to deliver a formal presentation of their complete idea to the core judging panel.

### Rules & Regulations
1. **Work & Development:** All brainstorming must be done at the venue. Teams can use their laptops and internet for research, but the core idea must be developed on the spot. Plagiarism leads to disqualification.
2. **Mentor Round Guidelines:** Teams should be prepared to quickly explain their approach, target audience, and feasibility. The mentor's shortlist decision is final.
3. **Final Presentation & Evaluation:** Shortlisted teams will have limited time (e.g., 5-7 minutes) to present, followed by Q&A. Evaluated on innovation, practical feasibility, impact, and clarity.

## 5. Mock Placement Drive
* **Eligibility:** V Semester Students Only
* **Participation:** Individual (1 person)
* **Venue:** Room 306
* **Format:** Group Discussion and Personal Interview

### Event Flow
* **Round 1 (Group Discussion):** Participants are divided into small batches. Each group discusses a given technical or general topic for 10-15 minutes. Evaluators observe and shortlist candidates.
* **Round 2 (Personal Interview):** Shortlisted candidates undergo a one-on-one technical and HR interview with the judging panel.

### Rules & Regulations
1. **Professional Attire & Documentation:** Strict formal dress code is mandatory. Carry at least two printed hard copies of an updated resume/CV.
2. **Group Discussion Conduct:** Maintain professional decorum. Interrupting aggressively or unprofessional language leads to negative marking or disqualification. Evaluation based on communication, leadership, logic, and active listening.
3. **Interview Guidelines:** Wait quietly outside Room 306 until called. Evaluation focuses on technical fundamentals, problem-solving, confidence, and presentation.

## 6. Poster Competition
* **Theme:** E-Scrap
* **Eligibility:** All Semesters
* **Team Size:** Maximum 2 members per team
* **Venue:** Room 301
* **Format:** Single-Round Exhibition

### Event Flow
* **Reporting & Setup:** Teams mount or display their pre-made posters at designated workstations.
* **Live Evaluation:** The judging panel visits each poster sequentially. Teams get 3-5 minutes to explain their concept, followed by a Q&A session.

### Rules & Regulations
1. **Poster Specifications:** Posters must be completely prepared in advance (standard A3/A2 or chart paper). Can be hand-drawn or digitally designed and printed.
2. **Theme Adherence & Originality:** Must strictly revolve around "E-Scrap" (electronic waste). The artwork must be original. Plagiarism leads to immediate disqualification.

## 7. E-Gaming (Free Fire & BGMI)
* **Games:** Free Fire and BGMI
* **Eligibility:** All Semesters
* **Team Size:** Squad of 4 members per team
* **Venue:** Rooms 307 and 302
* **Format:** Custom Room Matches

### Event Flow
* **Reporting & Room Allocation:** Squads report to their rooms. Custom room IDs/Passwords are shared exclusively with team leaders at the venue.
* **Qualifying Matches (If needed):** Top-performing teams advance based on placement and kill points.
* **Final Showdown:** Qualifying squads compete in final match(es) to determine the champion.

### Rules & Regulations
1. **Device & Network Requirements:** Smartphones only (No tablets, iPads, or emulators). Players must use their own mobile data internet.
2. **Fair Play & Anti-Cheat:** Hacks, mods, aimbots, GFX tools, or physical triggers are strictly banned. Players must use native touch controls. Cheating results in permanent squad disqualification.
3. **In-Game Rules:** Teaming up with rival squads is prohibited. Players must download required maps beforehand. Point system decided by coordinators is absolute.
4. **Conduct:** No toxic behavior, shouting, or abusive language. Remain seated until the match concludes.

## 8. Open Mic Competition
* **Eligibility:** All Semesters
* **Venue:** Aeronautical Seminar Hall (Room 303)
* **Format:** Single-Round Live Performance

### Event Flow
* **Reporting & Audio Setup:** Participants report prior to start time. Karaoke/backing tracks (MP3 on pen drive) must be submitted to coordinators at least 30 mins to 1 hour prior.
* **Live Performances:** Participants perform sequentially (singing, comedy, poetry, beatboxing, etc.).

### Rules & Regulations
1. **Performance Time Limit:** Strict time limit of 4 to 5 minutes per performer. Exceeding time leads to point deductions or audio cut.
2. **Content & Decorum:** Must maintain standard decency. Offensive language, hate speech, or inappropriate themes result in immediate disqualification and removal from stage.
3. **Equipment & Instruments:** Standard microphones and basic sound system provided. Participants must bring their own musical instruments if required.
4. **Evaluation Criteria:** Evaluated on confidence, stage presence, crowd engagement, voice/delivery quality, and overall entertainment value.
